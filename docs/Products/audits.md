# Audits

## Overview

At Emmet.Finance, security is a top priority. To ensure the integrity and reliability of our smart contracts, we undergo rigorous audits conducted by trusted third-party security firms. These audits are designed to identify and mitigate vulnerabilities, ensuring that our platform operates securely and efficiently for all users.

- [TON bridge](https://github.com/Emmet-Finance/audits/blob/main/ton/Coinvent%20(Bridge).pdf)

## Ongoing Security Practices

In addition to third-party audits, Emmet.Finance employs the following measures to maintain robust security:

Continuous Testing: Regularly running extensive test suites to validate new updates.

Bug Bounty Program: Encouraging the community to identify and report vulnerabilities.

Internal Reviews: Conducting routine internal code reviews for all new features and updates.

## Conclusion

Emmet.Finance is committed to providing a secure and reliable platform for decentralized finance. Through rigorous audits and ongoing security practices, we aim to uphold the trust and confidence of our users.
